Born in 2012, Eric discovered hip-hop at just 10 years old, heavily inspired by Eminem and the legends of the old school thang. Also, at the same age he came up with his artistic name: Frenper. That's how the path started...

By 2024, he dropped his debut album "The M.A.D" in Russian language. In 2025, Eric connected with his brother from another mother and musical mentor Tinashe, leading to their first collab track. Now, in 2026 they've officially joined forces to build the music to another level, co-founding their independent label: Frenper Sound Records.
